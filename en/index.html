Free Coloring Pages to Download and Print

KidsLoveColor offers a large collection of free printable coloring pages for kids. Whether you're looking for animal coloring pages, vehicle coloring pages, princess coloring pages or seasonal coloring pages — you'll find them all here. Click on a coloring page, press Print or Download, and start right away.

Our coloring pages are designed for children of all ages: from simple coloring sheets for toddlers to more detailed designs for older children. All coloring pages are 100% free and available instantly — no registration or payment required.

Popular categories of coloring pages

  • Animal coloring pages — cat, dog, butterfly, elephant, fish and more
  • Vehicle coloring pages — car, bicycle, airplane, sailboat
  • Princess coloring pages — princess, fairy and fairytale figures
  • Seasonal coloring pages — flower, snowman, sun, autumn leaf

Tips for parents

  • Print on A4/Letter for best quality
  • Use colored pencils, markers or crayons
  • Hang the best coloring page on the fridge!
  • Keep them in a folder as a keepsake

Our story — started at the kitchen table

One mother, one daughter and a whole lot of colored pencils.

Kids Love Color began with something very simple: imagining together what we would love to color ourselves. At the kitchen table, little ideas slowly grew into a website full of animals, worlds and stories that everyone can print for free.
